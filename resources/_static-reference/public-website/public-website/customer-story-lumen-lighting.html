Lumen Lighting recovers 1 in 5 abandoned carts

PNWritten by Priya Nair · Edited by Maya OrtizUpdated Sep 2026Illustrative story
21%carts recovered
+£48Kextra monthly sales
12flows live
In short

A cart recovery flow and live chat on product pages turned hesitation into orders.

About Lumen Lighting

Lumen sells designer lamps and lighting. Shoppers often hesitate on bulb types and delivery times.

The challenge

Visitors abandoned carts with unanswered questions about compatibility and shipping.

The solution

  1. Cart recovery flow. A friendly message answers the likely question and links back to checkout.
  2. Chat on product pages. Compatibility questions get answered while shoppers browse.
  3. Discount rules. Offers appear only for carts above a set value.

The results

Recovered carts became a steady share of monthly revenue, and the flow required no developer time.

“The flow paid for itself in the first month.”
Sofia Marino, Ecommerce Lead, Lumen Lighting
